      04 — Case study
      
      How I built the payroll suite
      
      
      
        One project, written up properly: the problem, the design decisions
        that mattered, and how the thing is put together.
      
      
    
      

    
      
      
      
        
      The problem
      
        
      
          My first tool calculated one employee's take-home pay. That is
          the easy half. The half that actually keeps a business in
          trouble is the employer's side: the forms, the deadlines, and
          the cases that break the simple maths — an employee who joined
          in November, a month with a bonus, a commission earner who is
          not a UIF contributor.
        
      

        
      The decision that mattered
      
        
      
          The interesting problem was the tax itself. SARS's brackets are
          built around a year of income, so a month cannot be
          taxed on its own. The engine annualises the month's taxable
          income, taxes that, and divides by twelve — which is what a
          real payroll does, and what smooths out bonuses and gaps in
          overtime.
        
      
        
      
          The consequence is subtle: an employee who joins in November
          is taxed as if they would earn a full year, so the employer
          over-deducts. A real system fixes that on the last payslip of
          the year, so the suite has a year-end reconciliation
          that works out the employee's real liability and the
          adjustment the employer owes them.
        
      

        
        
          
      • Section 11F: the pension is capped at 27.5% of the remuneration and R350 000 a year
        • 
          
      • Overtime at 1.5x for the first 45 hours, 2x after that, 2x for Sundays and public holidays
        • 
          
      • PAYE rounded to the nearest rand, the way SARS asks for it
        • 
          
      • Deadlines that know the 7th of the month is sometimes a Saturday
        • 
          
      • Every figure traceable back to a SARS source
        •
